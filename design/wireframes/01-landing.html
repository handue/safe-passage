Practice leaving home before you have to.

Enter your address. Run a simulated wildfire. Find what slows your household down, fix it, and run the same fire again.

Try demo address Boulder Creek Library, CA, with a sample household. Nothing to type.

Your address is sent to map, route, terrain and weather services to build the drill. The AI writer gets road names and times, not your house number.

  1. 1. Rehearse

    A practice fire spreads with wind and slope near your home.

  2. 2. Find weak spots

    One way out, a road the fire closes, or the task that takes longest.

  3. 3. Fix and compare

    Change the plan and see before and after on the same fire.

This is not a fire alert app. It does not track live fires or tell you when to leave.
