How this plan was made

Three kinds of information appear in Safe Passage:

CALCULATED worked out by code from data.

YOUR ESTIMATE a number you gave or accepted.

AI-WRITTEN wording by a language model. It does not set times or routes.

How the numbers are worked out

Fire: our own grid model. Each step, fire can move to nearby cells; wind and uphill slope make that more likely. The same seed gives the same fire.

Time needed: your task minutes, one after another, plus drive time to the last point the fire can cut your route.

Time to spare: the minute that point closes, minus time needed.

What this model leaves out

  • Vegetation, fuel moisture, humidity, embers jumping ahead, and firefighting.
  • Traffic, crashes, smoke on the road, and downed power lines.
  • Wind changes during a drill.
  • Whether a destination is open or has room.
  • Delays between a fire starting and an order reaching you.

Real fires can move much faster than this model.
